Follow the [installation instructions for your platform](/guide/installing/), then open `http://localhost:6633` on the machine running Nodearr. From another machine, use the server's address and port 6633 instead. Choose a username and password when prompted. **Home** then guides you through connecting your Arr apps, naming your node and pairing with a friend.

Older versions are on the [Releases](https://github.com/Nodearr/Nodearr/releases) page. Nodearr does not update itself: install a new version over the old one, and your settings and friends are kept.

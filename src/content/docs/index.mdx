<Aside type="note" title="Pre-release">
Everything Nodearr set out to do for its first version works, and has been run between machines on different home networks with no port forwarded. It has not yet been lived with for weeks. See [what is still open](https://github.com/Nodearr/Nodearr/blob/main/docs/plans/v1-implementation-plan.md#what-is-still-open).
</Aside>

When your [Sonarr](https://sonarr.tv/) wants an episode a friend has, it finds it in Nodearr, grabs it, and imports it like any other download. The file comes straight from your friend's machine to yours, and is checked against its hash before your Arr app sees it.

Each person runs Nodearr on their own machine, chooses what to share and can revoke trust at any time. You do not need a Nodearr account, and the project receives no copy of your library. Public relays help nodes connect, but downloaded media travels directly between friends. See [Privacy and security](/guide/privacy-and-security/) for what friends and relay operators can see.

## What it works with

| App | What Nodearr does with it |
| --- | --- |
| Sonarr | Shares whole series, seasons or single episodes. Finds episodes by TVDB, TMDb or IMDb ID, by scene numbering, and by air date for daily shows. |
| [Radarr](https://radarr.video/) | Finds movies by TMDb or IMDb ID. |
| [Lidarr](https://lidarr.audio/) | Shares whole artists or single albums, and moves an album as a folder of tracks. |
| Whisparr v3 | Reads films and scenes. Finds films by ID and scenes by studio, release date, title or code. |
| Whisparr v2 | Reads sites and scenes. Finds scenes by studio, release date, title or code, including scenes shared by v3 users. |
| [Prowlarr](https://prowlarr.com/) | Adds Nodearr as an indexer, so you can search it there with your others. |

It runs where your Arr apps run: Windows, Linux, macOS, and [Docker](https://docs.docker.com/) for amd64 and arm64. Most of it is built from [Sonarr](https://github.com/Sonarr/Sonarr), so it looks and works the way you expect.
